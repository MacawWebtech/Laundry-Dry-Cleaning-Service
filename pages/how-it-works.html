The process

How FreshFold works

Four simple steps — and you only do the first one. Here's exactly what happens between your door and your wardrobe.

  1. 1

    Schedule

  2. 2

    We pick up

  3. 3

    We clean

  4. 4

    We deliver

1
Step 1

Schedule your pickup

Book on the website, in your dashboard or on WhatsApp. Choose a service, a 2-hour pickup window and when you'd like it back. You'll get an SMS and email confirmation instantly.

  • Slots from 7 AM to 10 PM, 7 days a week
  • No need to count or weigh anything
  • Reschedule free up to 1 hour before
2
Step 2

We pick up from your door

Your rider calls 15 minutes before arriving. Hand over your clothes in any bag — we'll swap it for a FreshFold laundry bag and give you a printed receipt.

  • Contactless pickup available — leave it with security
  • Live rider location in your dashboard
  • First order? You'll get free reusable bags
3
Step 3

We clean with care

At our facility, every item is tagged, sorted by fabric and colour, stain-treated and cleaned in your own load. Dry cleaning goes to a separate specialist line.

  • Weight and item count sent to you before cleaning
  • Eco-safe detergents, hydrocarbon dry cleaning
  • Two-person quality check on every order
4
Step 4

We deliver it back fresh

Clothes come back folded, pressed or on hangers, sealed in a tagged bag. Pay online or to the rider, and rate your order to help us improve.

  • Delivered in 24–48 hours
  • Shirts on hangers, rest folded by category
  • Not happy? Free re-clean, no questions asked
