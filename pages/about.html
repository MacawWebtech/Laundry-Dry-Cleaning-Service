Our story

We give you your weekends back

FreshFold started in 2018 with one washing machine, two scooters and a promise: laundry that's on time, honestly priced and done with care.

How it started

From one machine in HSR Layout to 18,000 homes

Neha and Vikram were working 60-hour weeks and spending every Sunday on laundry. The local options were slow, unpredictable and rarely picked up the phone. So they rented a 300 sq ft shop, bought a commercial washer and started collecting laundry from their neighbours.

Eight years later we run a 6,000 sq ft facility, 40 riders and a team of garment-care specialists — but we still treat every bag like it belongs to a neighbour.

Mission

Clean clothes, zero effort, kinder to the planet

On time, every time

97.6% of orders were delivered in their promised slot last year. If we're late, your next pickup is free.

Lighter footprint

Our machines recycle 40% of rinse water, our riders use electric scooters, and our bags are reusable.

Fair to our team

We invest in our people with stable employment, ongoing support, and a workplace built on respect.
