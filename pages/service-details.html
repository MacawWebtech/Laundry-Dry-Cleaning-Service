Our process

Five careful steps for every garment

Dry cleaning isn't really "dry" — it means cleaning without water. We pick the method per fabric so nothing shrinks, fades or loses shape.

Fabrics we handle

  • Silk & satin
  • Wool & cashmere
  • Linen
  • Velvet
  • Chiffon & georgette
  • Leather trims
  • Zari & embroidery
  • Sequins & beadwork
  • Rayon & viscose
  • Suede (spot clean)
1

Inspect & tag

Every item gets a barcode tag. We note stains, missing buttons and loose threads, and photograph any existing damage.

2

Pre-treat stains

Stain specialists treat each mark by type — oil, protein, tannin or dye — using fabric-specific spotting agents.

3

Gentle clean

Garments are cleaned in a hydrocarbon or wet-clean cycle at controlled temperatures — no harsh perc.

4

Finish & press

Hand-finished on steam forms and pressing tables. Pleats, lapels and creases are set exactly.

5

Quality check & bag

A second person checks every item under bright light before it's bagged, hung and sent out.
